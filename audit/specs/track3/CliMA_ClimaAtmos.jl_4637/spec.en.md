## CliMA/ClimaAtmos.jl#4637 Fixes problems near upper boundary and tracer-mass continuity

- What changed (1–3 sentences, naming files and formulas):
  `src/cache/precomputed_quantities.jl` changes the top boundary condition to the covariant component `u₃ = -uₕ³/g³³`, so that the total contravariant component `u³ = uₕ³ + g³³u₃` is zero; the continuity equation in `src/prognostic_equations/implicit/implicit_tendency.jl` now uses the same zero-boundary-flux divergence `ᶜadvdivᵥ` as the tracers. `advection.jl` restores the grid-mean vertical advection of all tracers found, `edmfx_sgs_flux.jl` changes the SGS correction to the flux divergence of `ρᵏaᵏ(u³ᵏ−u³)(χᵏ−χ)` and adds the total-water SGS mass tendency to the moist-air density as well; `implicit/manual_sparse_jacobian.jl` is changed to an approximate linearization of the updraft that matches the central reconstruction, omitting the environment terms. `remaining_tendency.jl` only adds a TODO to the existing condensate sponge layer and does not fix that term.

- Invariants (mathematical statements; write them as checkable equalities/inequalities, give variables, sign conventions, domain of validity):
  1. **Primary — impermeable boundaries and closed vertical mass budget.** Let the vertical coordinate point upward, `u₃` be the covariant velocity, `uₕ³` the projection of the horizontal wind onto the contravariant vertical direction, and the positive-definite grid metric satisfy `g³³ > 0`. In the non-prescribed-flow mode, after boundary filtering is complete, the top and bottom faces should satisfy `u³_b = uₕ³_b + g³³_b u₃_b = 0`; every prognostic EDMFX subdomain that shares the horizontal velocity should also satisfy `u³ʲ_b = 0`. For the grid-mean vertical mass advection alone, `Aρ = −D₀ Fρ`, where `Fρ_f = I(ρJ_c)_f u³_f/J_f`, `D₀` specifies zero flux at both ends and `I` is face interpolation, we should have `Σ_i V_i Aρ_i = 0`, where `V_i > 0` is the physical cell volume including the geometric and horizontal quadrature weights. The zero flux must hold structurally in the divergence operator, even if the cached end-face velocity is artificially given a nonzero value. This excludes free precipitation outflow, surface sources and sinks, and other tendencies.
  2. **Primary — a constant tracer is consistent with mass advection, and the SGS term produces no flux from zero contrast.** For any specific tracer `χ`, let `ρχ_i = Cρ_i` with `C` a constant; then the grid-mean vertical advection should satisfy `A(ρχ)_i = C Aρ_i` pointwise, including the first and last cells. If all updrafts and the diagnostic environment also satisfy `χᵏ_i = χ_i = C`, then the SGS correction alone, `Sχ = −D₀ Σ_k Fχᵏ`, should be zero; in continuous form `Fχᵏ = ρᵏaᵏ(u³ᵏ−u³)(χᵏ−χ)`, and the discrete form should follow the actual reconstruction `aᵏ(χᵏ−χ)`, without assuming that the product of interpolations equals the interpolation of the product. This property allows the density, area fraction and velocity to vary with height, and also allows the grid-mean and subdomain densities to differ. It is an identity of the split operators: when the total-water SGS flux is nonzero, one cannot claim from it that an arbitrary uniform passive tracer stays uniform in the full moist-air equations, because the denominator ρ also receives the water mass tendency.
  3. **Secondary — SGS water mass and moist-air mass are transported in pairs.** `ρ` is the moist-air mass density, `q_t` the total-water mass fraction, and `ρ_d = ρ−ρq_t` the dry-air density. When only `edmfx_sgs_mass_flux_tendency!` is switched on and its increment extracted, we should have pointwise `Sρ = S(ρq_t)`, hence `Sρ_d = 0`; with closed vertical boundaries we should also have `Σ_i V_i Sρ_i = Σ_i V_i S(ρq_t)_i = 0`. This expresses the convention of this library that treats this SGS water term as a redistribution of water mass, and does not claim that the local dry-air tendency of all turbulent processes is zero. Mass fractions are dimensionless and mass tendencies are in kg m⁻³ s⁻¹; if a number-concentration tracer uses particles per unit mass, its tendency has a correspondingly different unit and must not be added to the water mass.

- Sources of the invariants (first principles / literature formula / consistency with other implementations in the library / dimensions):
  Invariant 1 comes from `u·n = 0` at a fixed wall, the coordinate transformation, and the discrete divergence theorem; invariant 2 comes from the multiplicative identity between the continuity equation and the transport of a constant scalar, and from the covariance flux being zero when the scalar has no subdomain contrast. Neither depends on any empirical turbulence parameter. Invariant 3 comes from the moist-air composition identity and consistency with the library's existing treatment of water diffusion; the merged [SGS flux implementation and the corresponding diffusion term](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CliMA/ClimaAtmos.jl/a8efce5a0040924094a58739db91a0f3162f779c/src/prognostic_equations/edmfx_sgs_flux.jl) provides both paths. The geometric boundary, the zero-flux divergence and the reconstruction rely on `precomputed_quantities.jl` and `utils/abbreviations.jl` in the input diff, and on the merged [vertical transport implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CliMA/ClimaAtmos.jl/a8efce5a0040924094a58739db91a0f3162f779c/src/prognostic_equations/implicit/implicit_tendency.jl). These are first-principles/implementation-convention derivations; the numerical-improvement descriptions in the PR are not taken as independent verification evidence.

- Common operating domain (range of each input, with the basis):
  The mathematical domain is finite, positive `ρ, ρᵏ, J_c, J_f, g³³`, finite velocities, and a non-degenerate grid; `0 ≤ aᵏ ≤ 1`, and the diagnostic environment mass `ρa⁰ = ρ−Σ_j ρʲaʲ > 0`. For random routine spot checks, a positive-definite, well-conditioned grid is recommended, with physical air density 0.1–1.5 kg m⁻³, temperature 230–310 K, horizontal wind −30–30 m s⁻¹, interior vertical velocity −5–5 m s⁻¹, one updraft with area fraction 0.01–0.2, and an environment mass fraction of at least 0.5; samples must be constructed from a consistent state, and samples violating thermodynamic/area constraints must be rejected; density, temperature and pressure must not be assigned independently at random. These are recommended mild atmospheric sampling ranges, not validity domains declared by the code.
  For water, take `0 ≤ q_t ≤ 0.03`, with every condensate nonnegative and their sum not exceeding `q_t`; use `C = 0, 10⁻⁶, 10⁻³` for the uniform scalar respectively, and `C = 1` only as an algebraic probe of the independent transport operator; one must not set all water species to 1 simultaneously and then reinitialize it as a physical thermodynamic state. Number concentrations may be supplemented with nonnegative constants on a valid 2M state, which is not a required baseline of this specification.
  The baselines with a source come from the newly added tests: Schar terrain, Linear mesh warping, 100 km horizontal domain/8 cells, 21 km height/20 layers, 500 m bottom layer, 10 m s⁻¹ initial wind, dt=1 s; the SGS baseline is DYCOMS_RF01, 1.5 km/30 equal-thickness layers, prognostic_edmfx, 1M, one updraft, ARS222, dt=120 s. Prefer small-perturbation and 20/40/80-layer spot checks on these configurations; additional explicit stepping should satisfy an actual local vertical Courant number ≤0.2, which is an audit control condition and not a stability guarantee of the PR. Float64 is primary, with Float32 as a re-check; SGS uses the default central reconstruction, supplemented by the source-supported first_order/third_order/vanleer_limiter (where the configuration allows). Exclude contamination of the checked increments by prescribed flow, sources and sinks, sponge layers, phase changes, sedimentation and diffusion.

- Formalization (Lean) feasibility: can the invariant be written as a proposition over finite-dimensional reals, and what are the main obstacles:
  **Yes.** Fix a finite grid, write the velocity, density, scalar, area, metric and quadrature weights as finite-dimensional real vectors, and D₀ and I as matrices: `g³³>0` proves the projection cancellation, `VᵀD₀` taking only boundary fluxes together with zero end faces proves that the mass sum is zero, a constant-preserving reconstruction/zero-input reconstruction proves `A(Cρ)=CAρ` and the SGS zero value, and the same increment proves `S(ρ−ρq_t)=0`. The main obstacles are mapping ClimaCore's geometric quadrature and the concrete reconstructions to matrix theorems, formalizing the mass-weighted relation of the environment diagnostics and the regularization branches, distinguishing the finite-difference boundary from the sedimentation boundary, and separately proving floating-point error bounds; the real-number identities themselves do not prove that the Julia cache scheduling, the approximate Jacobian or the time integration is correct.
